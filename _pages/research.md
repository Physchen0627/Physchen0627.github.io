## Research

<div class="section-card">

### Quantum Error Correction

Quantum error correction is essential for scalable quantum computation, as quantum systems are inherently vulnerable to noise. While surface codes have long been a leading approach to fault-tolerant quantum computing, their considerable qubit overhead has motivated the development of more resource-efficient alternatives.

My research focuses on **quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) codes**. In the stabilizer framework, qLDPC codes are characterized by sparse check structures, where each stabilizer acts on only a constant number of qubits and each qubit participates in only a constant number of stabilizer checks. I am particularly interested in their theoretical properties, fault-tolerant implementation, and potential advantages for scalable quantum computation.

### Superconducting Quantum Circuits

**Superconducting quantum circuits** are one of the leading hardware platforms for quantum computing.

My current research investigates quantum error correction in superconducting architectures, with particular emphasis on the interplay between qLDPC codes and realistic hardware constraints. Topics of interest include qubit connectivity, circuit-level noise, and the practical implementation of fault-tolerant error-correction protocols.
